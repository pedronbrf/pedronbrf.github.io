About

Connecting development programs with expertise.

I am a PharmD and Business Development Manager at Astrum CRO, working with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies across the clinical development landscape.

My role is focused on understanding development needs and connecting sponsors with the right clinical research capabilities across different stages of the development journey.

While Phase I and Clinical Pharmacology are a key area of focus, my work spans early and later-stage clinical development, including Phase II–III, late-phase services and RWE.

Full-service CRO delivering Phase I–IV clinical development and RWE, supported by our 64-bed hospital-based Phase I Unit in Porto and operational expertise across Europe and beyond.
